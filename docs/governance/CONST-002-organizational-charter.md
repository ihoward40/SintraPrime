## Constitutional Scope

This charter defines SintraPrime's organizational structure, authority boundaries, and structural invariants only. It does not define operating procedures, staffing assignments, schedules, templates, or runbooks. Those are delegated to subordinate manuals and playbooks.

## Article OC-001 — Charter Scope and Purity

**Traceability**
- Implements: CONST-001 constitutional supremacy and document hierarchy intent
- Implemented By: Department Handbook, Agent Handbook, Approval Matrix, Mission Cycle Framework
- Verified By: GOV-000, GRF review, constitutional maturity assessment

### Purpose
Define the single constitutional question answered by this artifact: how the enterprise may be organized and governed structurally.

### Constitutional Rule
This charter shall answer organizational structure and authority questions only. Operational procedure, workflow timing, KPI formulas, staffing rosters, and execution templates shall not be embedded in this artifact.

### Delegation
Subordinate manuals and playbooks may define procedures that implement this charter, provided they do not alter constitutional structure or authority.

### Enforcement
Any section proposed for this charter that introduces operational procedure shall be treated as a constitutional defect and removed or delegated before approval.

### Compliance Metric
100% of normative statements in this artifact remain structural or authority-defining, with zero embedded runbook steps.

### Commentary
This article applies the Constitutional Purity Rule: one artifact answers one question only.

## Article OC-002 — Enterprise Organizational Model

**Traceability**
- Implements: CONST-001 enterprise governance baseline
- Implemented By: Department Handbook, Agent Handbook, Business Unit registry, Team registry
- Verified By: GOV-000, organizational inventory review

### Purpose
Define the constitutional model for enterprise organizational relationships.

### Constitutional Rule
The constitutional organizational model is:

`Enterprise -> Business Unit -> Department -> Function -> Team -> Agent`

Humans may hold authority-bearing offices at any level permitted by governing artifacts, but every office, team, and agent shall resolve to exactly one enterprise chain of authority.

### Delegation
Subordinate artifacts may name specific business units, departments, teams, and agents, but shall not create alternative structural layers without constitutional amendment.

### Enforcement
Units or agents that cannot be placed within a valid constitutional chain shall be non-authoritative and may not receive delegated decision rights.

### Compliance Metric
100% of active organizational entities map to exactly one valid parent chain from Enterprise to Agent.

### Commentary
Functions may exist with or without permanent teams, but they remain subordinate to departments and may not bypass department accountability.

## Article OC-003 — Authority Classes and Direction of Accountability

**Traceability**
- Implements: CONST-001 authority and approval boundary concepts
- Implemented By: Approval Matrix, RACI Matrix, Agent Handbook
- Verified By: GOV-000, authority review, exception log review

### Purpose
Define authority classes and the constitutional direction of authority and responsibility.

### Constitutional Rule
Authority shall flow downward by delegation; responsibility shall flow upward by accountability. The enterprise recognizes the following authority classes:

1. **Constitutional Authority** — authority created only by ratified Tier 1 artifacts.
2. **Reserved Enterprise Authority** — authority retained at enterprise or business unit leadership level.
3. **Delegated Department Authority** — authority assigned to a department within a defined scope.
4. **Delegated Functional or Team Authority** — authority assigned below the department level.
5. **Agent Authority** — revocable authority limited to explicitly delegated scope, evidence, and controls.
6. **Advisory Authority** — non-binding authority that may inform but not compel action.

No lower authority class may enlarge its own scope, supersede a higher authority source, or approve its own constitutional exception.

### Delegation
Approval matrices, role manuals, and agent governance artifacts may specify which decisions sit in each class and how delegations are recorded.

### Enforcement
Any decision taken without traceable source authority, or beyond delegated scope, shall be voidable and subject to review escalation.

### Compliance Metric
100% of material delegations identify a source authority and accountable superior; zero orphan or self-originated delegations remain unresolved.

### Commentary
Downward authority enables action; upward responsibility preserves accountability.

## Article OC-004 — Department Classes

**Traceability**
- Implements: CONST-001 organizational governance direction
- Implemented By: Department Handbook, business unit charters, organizational registry
- Verified By: GOV-000, charter review, organizational classification review

### Purpose
Define the constitutional classes of departments without binding the enterprise to current personnel or agents.

### Constitutional Rule
Departments shall be classified by enduring purpose, not by incumbent, tool, or named agent. Recognized classes include:

- Governance
- Strategy
- Standards
- Security
- Engineering
- Operations
- Quality Assurance
- Audit
- Revenue
- Finance
- Legal and Compliance
- Customer Success
- Research

The enterprise may add or retire department classes only through constitutional amendment or explicit authorization from a governing Tier 1 artifact.

### Delegation
Subordinate artifacts may instantiate, describe, or temporarily leave vacant departments within these classes.

### Enforcement
Departments created without an approved class, or assigned mutually incompatible classes without explicit constitutional authority, shall not receive standing authority.

### Compliance Metric
100% of active departments carry one primary constitutional class and an identified parent business unit.

### Commentary
This article classifies what kinds of departments may exist; it does not assign named people, agents, or current headcount.

## Article OC-005 — Independence Requirements

**Traceability**
- Implements: CONST-001 separation-of-duties principle
- Implemented By: Department Handbook, Approval Matrix, Security Policy, RACI Matrix
- Verified By: GOV-000, GRF review, independence attestation review

### Purpose
Define required structural independence boundaries.

### Constitutional Rule
The following independence boundaries are mandatory unless an amended constitutional artifact states otherwise:

- Quality Assurance shall be organizationally distinct from Standards.
- Security shall be organizationally distinct from Engineering.
- Audit shall be organizationally distinct from Operations.
- Governance shall be organizationally distinct from Revenue.
- Any exception-reviewing function shall be distinct from the function seeking the exception.

Organizational distinctness requires separate authority lines, separate accountability, and independent review rights.

### Delegation
Subordinate artifacts may define specific reporting relationships and compensating controls, but may not collapse a required independence boundary.

### Enforcement
Structures that violate a required independence boundary shall be ineligible for final approval authority over the affected domain.

### Compliance Metric
Zero approved organizational structures contain an unresolved required-boundary conflict.

### Commentary
Independence is constitutional because it preserves objective review and prevents authority concentration.

## Article OC-006 — Department Lifecycle Events

**Traceability**
- Implements: CONST-001 change-governance direction
- Implemented By: Department Handbook, change-control records, Approval Matrix
- Verified By: GOV-000, organizational change log, GRF review

### Purpose
Define the constitutional lifecycle events by which departments change over time.

### Constitutional Rule
The only constitutional lifecycle events for departments are:

- Create
- Modify
- Merge
- Split
- Suspend
- Retire

Every lifecycle event shall preserve traceability of authority, accountability, inherited obligations, and successor state.

### Delegation
Subordinate manuals may define the review sequence, forms, and evidence required for each lifecycle event.

### Enforcement
Any unrecorded structural change, or any change that leaves authority or obligations without a successor or disposition, shall be non-effective.

### Compliance Metric
100% of department lifecycle events identify predecessor, successor or disposition, effective authority date, and accountable approver.

### Commentary
This article establishes the allowed event types only; it does not define event workflow.

## Article OC-007 — Organizational Invariants

**Traceability**
- Implements: CONST-001 constitutional control intent
- Implemented By: Department Handbook, Agent Handbook, Approval Matrix, GOV-000
- Verified By: GRF review, organizational conformity review, maturity assessment

### Purpose
Define the structural conditions that must remain true across all organizational states.

### Constitutional Rule
The enterprise shall maintain the following invariants:

1. Every authority-bearing unit has exactly one constitutional parent.
2. No organizational reporting chain may be circular.
3. Every department belongs to exactly one business unit at a time.
4. Every team or function belongs to exactly one department at a time.
5. Every agent operates under exactly one accountable organizational chain at a time.
6. No unit may both execute and finally attest its own independent review where independence is constitutionally required.
7. Temporary initiatives, missions, councils, and working groups do not become departments unless formally chartered.
8. Organizational naming may change without altering constitutional class, authority, or obligations unless explicitly stated.

### Delegation
Supporting artifacts may define inventories, labels, and evidence models used to prove these invariants.

### Enforcement
Any state that violates an invariant shall require remediation or explicit constitutional exception before it may be treated as authoritative.

### Compliance Metric
Zero unresolved invariant violations in the authoritative organizational inventory.

### Commentary
These invariants stabilize the enterprise even as subordinate operating models evolve.
