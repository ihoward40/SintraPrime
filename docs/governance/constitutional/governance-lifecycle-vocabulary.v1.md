# Governance Lifecycle Vocabulary v1

STATUS: Review
PURPOSE: Preserve the canonical lifecycle vocabulary for constitutional governance artifacts.

## Canonical States

- Draft — artifact is being prepared and may change materially.
- Review — artifact is under active evaluation and pending disposition.
- Approved — artifact content is accepted for planned use.
- Ratified — artifact is formally adopted into the constitutional stack.
- Enforced — artifact governs live behavior or binding enterprise posture.
- Archived — artifact is retained for history and is no longer active.

## Usage Note

Operational mission state labels such as `IN_PROGRESS` may appear in status reporting, but they do not replace the canonical lifecycle vocabulary for document disposition.
