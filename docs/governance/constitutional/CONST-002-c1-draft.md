## Draft Intent (Authority-First)

`CONST-002` defines organizational authority boundaries for SintraPrime governance execution readiness:

1. Departments are defined by mandate and authority, not personnel assignments.
2. Delegated authority is explicit, bounded, and revocable.
3. Disputes escalate through governance review authority.
4. Cross-department actions require traceability and approval evidence.

## Provisional Department Authority Map (C1)

- Governance Review Board: constitutional review authority and dispute resolution.
- Standards: consistency authority across artifacts and operating standards.
- QA: correctness verification authority over acceptance evidence.
- Operations: execution authority within approved governance boundaries.
- Automation/Agents: delegated operational execution only; no constitutional authority.
