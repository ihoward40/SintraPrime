# ADR-0001 — CONST-001 Freeze and Improvement Routing

Status: Active  
Date: 2026-07-02  
Owner: Governance Review Board

## Decision

`CONST-001` is frozen as the constitutional baseline and is not edited in-place during `CONST-002` readiness work.

## Freeze Behavior

- No direct modifications to `CONST-001` are permitted under this directive.
- Any proposed constitutional enhancement is routed through a Governance Request Form (GRF) entry.
- GRF entries are reviewed and dispositioned before any constitutional amendment cycle is opened.

## Consequence

- Constitutional continuity for reviewer handoff is preserved.
- Improvement proposals remain visible and auditable through the GRF register.
