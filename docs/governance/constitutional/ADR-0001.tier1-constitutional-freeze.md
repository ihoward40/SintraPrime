```yaml
Title:        Tier 1 Constitutional Freeze
Status:       Accepted

Deciders:     Executive Sponsor, Constitutional Reviewer
Scope:        CONST-001 and all Tier 1 constitutional artifacts
```

---

## Context

SintraPrime's Tier 1 constitutional layer (CONST-001 through CONST-004) forms the foundational authority structure for all governance, operational, and architectural decisions. CONST-001, the primary constitutional document, has been ratified and must not be subject to ad-hoc amendment during the active ratification of the remaining Tier 1 artifacts. Uncontrolled amendments during the Founding Week ratification cycle would undermine traceability, create conflicting constitutional authority, and compromise the governance baseline.

---

## Decision

**CONST-001 is frozen for the duration of Founding Week and until the Governance Baseline v1.0.0 tag is applied.**

All proposed amendments, corrections, or improvements to CONST-001 must be routed through the Governance Request Form (GRF) process and may only be incorporated after:

1. The triggering GRF is accepted by the Constitutional Reviewer.
2. The amendment is formally reviewed and approved as a constitutional amendment.
3. A new version of CONST-001 is issued and ratified before the amendment takes effect.

No direct edits to CONST-001 are permitted outside this process.

---

## ADR-0003 Reserved Slot

**ADR-0003 is permanently reserved and unassigned.**

No artifact, decision record, or governance document may be assigned to the ADR-0003 slot without a formal governance amendment process. This reservation is maintained to preserve the integrity of the ADR numbering sequence and to prevent conflicts with externally referenced ADR indices.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Constitutional authority is stable and unambiguous during Founding Week.
- All proposed changes are traceable through the GRF register.
- Amendment history is preserved and auditable.
- Cross-validation and ratification can be completed against a stable baseline.

### Negative / Trade-offs

- Minor improvements to CONST-001 cannot be incorporated immediately; they must wait for the formal amendment process.
- GRF volume may increase as reviewers identify improvements during the Tier 1 review cycle.

### Mitigations

- GRFs are lightweight and can be raised by any governance participant.
- GRF-C and GRF-D findings (non-blocking) are batched and addressed post-ratification.

---

## Amendment Routing

All proposed constitutional amendments to CONST-001 must follow this path:

```
Identify improvement or defect
    ↓
Raise GRF (classify: GRF-A, GRF-B, GRF-C, or GRF-D)
    ↓
Constitutional Reviewer evaluates GRF
    ↓
If GRF-A or GRF-B: schedule amendment review
    ↓
Draft constitutional amendment
    ↓
Amendment review and approval
    ↓
New CONST-001 version ratified
    ↓
GRF marked Resolved
```

Constitutional improvements must not bypass this process by direct edit.
