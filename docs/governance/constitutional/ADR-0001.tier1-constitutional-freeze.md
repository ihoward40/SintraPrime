## Context

SintraPrime Enterprise has completed the C2 review of CONST-001 (Enterprise
Constitution). The constitutional design phase of Founding Week is complete.
The enterprise is now transitioning from constitutional design to constitutional
implementation.

Without a formal freeze decision, CONST-001 risks ongoing iterative edits that
undermine the stability of the constitutional baseline. Implementing artifacts
(CONST-002 through CONST-004, REF-001) require a stable constitutional reference
to implement against. A moving constitutional target makes implementation incoherent.

## Decision

**CONST-001 is frozen at maturity level C2.**

All subsequent constitutional improvements are to be captured as **Governance Review
Findings (GRFs)** rather than direct edits to the frozen artifact. GRFs accumulate
for batch review at the next constitutional revision cycle.

The next constitutional revision cycle is triggered when all four Tier 1 artifacts
(CONST-001 through CONST-004) have reached C2 and the Constitutional Integration
Review has been completed.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Implementing artifacts (CONST-002 through CONST-004) have a stable reference.
- Governance drift from iterative constitutional edits is eliminated.
- The GRF process captures improvement intent without destabilizing the baseline.
- A clear trigger condition defines when the next revision cycle begins.

**Negative:**
- Known improvements to CONST-001 must wait for the next revision cycle.
- The GRF queue must be actively maintained to prevent findings from being lost.

**Neutral:**
- Governance Reviews of implementing artifacts may surface constitutional gaps;
  these are captured as GRFs and addressed in the next revision cycle.
