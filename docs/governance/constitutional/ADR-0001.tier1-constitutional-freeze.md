# ADR-0001 — Tier 1 Constitutional Freeze

STATUS: Approved
DATE: 2026-07-03

## Decision

Maintain the Founding Week documentation freeze until Tier 1 constitutional completion is achieved.

## Directives

- Do not expand governance scope beyond `CONST-002`, `CONST-003`, `CONST-004`, cross-validation, and ratification.
- Keep `ADR-0003` reserved only and not in active drafting.
- Preserve the hierarchy in which implementation documents are governed by constitutional and governance artifacts.
- Keep Track B in production-validation observation rather than feature expansion during the active watch window.

## Consequences

- Review effort stays concentrated on Tier 1 constitutional completion.
- New governance models, dashboards, or theories are deferred unless required to resolve a constitutional defect.
- Production observations may inform disposition, but they do not alter the frozen constitutional review path.
