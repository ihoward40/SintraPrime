# ADR-0001 — Tier 1 Constitutional Freeze

**Record ID:** ADR-0001  
**Type:** Architecture Decision Record  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-02  
**Authority:** Constitutional Reviewer & Governance Auditor  
**Affected Artifacts:** CONST-001, CONST-002, CONST-003, CONST-004  

---

## Context

SintraPrime Enterprise is in **Founding Week** — the initial constitutional establishment phase during which all Tier 1 artifacts are being drafted, reviewed, and ratified. The first Tier 1 artifact, **CONST-001** (Enterprise Constitution), has completed initial design and reached maturity **C2** (Governance Review Candidate).

Without a formal freeze mechanism, CONST-001 risks becoming a moving target: iterative refinement during active implementation creates instability across all implementing artifacts (CONST-002, CONST-003, CONST-004, MAN-XXX, PLAY-XXX), making constitutional conformance reviews impossible to anchor.

Mature governance programs separate the **design phase** from the **implementation phase** using a formal freeze. Once frozen, improvements are captured as Governance Review Findings (GRFs) and deferred to the next amendment cycle, preserving stability while maintaining a continuous improvement pathway.

---

## Decision

**CONST-001 (Enterprise Constitution, v1.0-r2) is frozen at maturity C2 effective immediately.**

Under this decision:

1. **No edits to CONST-001 are permitted** while this ADR is in active status.
2. **All identified improvements to CONST-001** must be documented as Governance Review Findings (GRFs) and filed in the GRF register. They will be reviewed during the next amendment cycle.
3. **CONST-001 serves as the stable constitutional baseline** for all Tier 1 artifact development during Founding Week.
4. **Implementing artifacts** (CONST-002 through CONST-004, MAN-XXX, PLAY-XXX) must implement CONST-001 faithfully without redefining, contradicting, or expanding it.
5. **Constitutional review of implementing artifacts** validates conformance to the frozen CONST-001 baseline.

### Freeze Scope

| Artifact | Freeze Status | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| CONST-001 | **Frozen at C2** | No edits permitted under this ADR |
| CONST-002 | Active C1 Development | Must implement CONST-001; no redefinition |
| CONST-003 | Planned (C0) | Must implement CONST-001; no redefinition |
| CONST-004 | Planned (C0) | Must implement CONST-001; no redefinition |

### GRF Pathway

Any party who identifies a defect, gap, or improvement opportunity in CONST-001 must:

1. Document the finding as a Governance Review Finding (GRF) using the standard GRF template.
2. File the GRF in the CONST-001 GRF Register.
3. Classify the GRF as Blocking or Non-Blocking.
4. Await the next constitutional amendment cycle for disposition.

Blocking GRFs (Class A defects) that cannot be deferred must trigger a formal amendment process with full constitutional review.

---

## Consequences

### Positive Consequences

- CONST-001 provides a stable, immutable baseline for all Founding Week implementation work.
- Constitutional conformance reviews have a fixed target, making them reproducible and consistent.
- Implementing artifacts cannot drift from the constitutional baseline during development.
- Continuous improvement is preserved via the GRF pathway without destabilizing the framework.

### Negative Consequences

- Identified improvements to CONST-001 cannot be incorporated immediately. They must wait for the next amendment cycle.
- If a Class A constitutional defect is discovered in CONST-001, a formal amendment process (with full review overhead) is required, which may extend Founding Week duration.

### Risk Mitigation

- The GRF register is initialized and maintained throughout Founding Week.
- The Constitutional Reviewer monitors incoming GRFs and classifies them promptly.
- Blocking GRFs that require immediate action trigger the amendment process with expedited (but complete) review.

---

## Review and Expiry

This ADR remains active until one of the following conditions is met:

1. **Founding Week completes** — all Tier 1 artifacts are ratified, GOV-000 traceability is complete, and the repository is tagged **Governance Baseline v1.0.0**.
2. **A Class A constitutional defect is discovered** — in which case this ADR is superseded by an emergency amendment record and the freeze is temporarily lifted for the specific defect.

Upon expiry, CONST-001 enters the Constitutional Integration Review phase prior to ratification.
