## 1. Context

Founding Week established a deterministic governance process for advancing Tier 1 constitutional artifacts through defined maturity stages. CONST-001 (Enterprise Constitution) is frozen at C2 and serves as the constitutional baseline for all downstream artifacts.

As additional Tier 1 artifacts (CONST-002, CONST-003, CONST-004) are developed and submitted for governance review, a repeatable and auditable review methodology is required. Without a governed review process, review outcomes depend on reviewer judgment rather than a defined protocol, making the process non-repeatable and non-auditable.

This ADR formalizes the Constitutional Review Protocol — the method by which constitutional artifacts are evaluated before maturity advancement. Governing the review method itself ensures that future reviews are consistent regardless of who performs them and provides a stable citation target for all review records.

---

## 2. Decision

The Constitutional Review Protocol described in this ADR is adopted as the mandatory review methodology for all Tier 1 artifacts during Founding Week and beyond. Every artifact submitted for governance review MUST be evaluated using this protocol in the order defined herein. No artifact may advance maturity without a completed review record issued under this protocol.

---

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 Artifacts Governed

This protocol governs reviews of all **Tier 1 constitutional artifacts**, defined as:

| Artifact  | Name                           | Constitutional Role                      |
| --------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| CONST-001 | Enterprise Constitution        | Constitutional baseline (frozen at C2)   |
| CONST-002 | Organizational Charter         | Organizational authority implementation  |
| CONST-003 | Governance Charter             | Governance authority structure           |
| CONST-004 | Standards Charter              | Standards independence and enforcement   |
| GOV-000   | Governance Traceability Matrix | Verification layer for all Tier 1 requirements |

### 3.2 Artifacts Not Governed

This protocol does not apply to:

- Tier 2 or lower operational artifacts
- Process or tooling documents that are not Tier 1 constitutional artifacts
- ADRs themselves (ADRs are not reviewed under this protocol; they are issued as governance decisions)

---

## 4. Rationale

### 4.1 Why Govern the Review Method

A governance process that is not itself governed is inherently unstable. If the review method changes between artifacts, review records cannot be compared or cited with confidence. Versioning the review protocol as an ADR ensures:

- All Tier 1 review records are produced by the same method.
- Any change to the review protocol requires a new ADR, creating an auditable version history.
- Reviews are traceable to a specific protocol version, not to ad hoc reviewer judgment.

### 4.2 Why a Six-Step Sequence

The six-step review sequence (§ 6) maps to five constitutional validity questions plus a disposition gate:

1. **Completeness** — Was the artifact package properly assembled?
2. **Constitutional Conformance** — Does it faithfully implement CONST-001?
3. **Structural Integrity** — Is its internal model coherent?
4. **Traceability** — Is it connected to the traceability graph?
5. **Governability** — Can it be enforced and measured?
6. **Disposition** — What is the formal outcome?

Combining these questions into a single gate or omitting any step creates blind spots that reduce governance assurance.

### 4.3 Why GRF Classification

Governance Review Findings (GRFs) require severity classification to distinguish findings that prevent maturity advancement (GRF-A) from those that require correction, improvement, or editorial clarification (GRF-B, GRF-C, GRF-D). Without classification, all findings carry equal weight, which either stalls governance on minor issues or allows critical defects to pass.

## 5. Evidence Hierarchy

All review conclusions MUST be supported by evidence drawn from the following hierarchy, in descending order of authority:

| Level | Source                                      | Authority                              |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| E1    | CONST-001 Enterprise Constitution (C2)      | Constitutional baseline — supreme      |
| E2    | Ratified Tier 1 artifacts (C3)              | Implementing constitutional authority  |
| E3    | GOV-000 Governance Traceability Matrix      | Traceability verification layer        |
| E4    | GRF Register for the artifact under review  | Finding record for current review cycle |
| E5    | Cross-reference validation report           | Cross-document consistency record      |
| E6    | Prior ADRs (ADR-0001 and successors)        | Governance decision record             |

A reviewer finding that cannot be supported by at least one evidence source at E1–E6 MUST be marked as informational and may not be issued as a GRF.

---

## 6. Mandatory Review Gates

Each Tier 1 artifact review MUST proceed through all six gates in the order listed. Gates may not be skipped or reordered. Each gate produces a pass/fail verdict; a failure at any gate is recorded as one or more GRFs with the appropriate severity classification.

### Gate 1 — Completeness Check

**Purpose:** Verify that the submission package is complete before substantive review begins.

| Check                                      | Pass Condition                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Required sections present                  | All mandatory sections per artifact type present   |
| Metadata complete                          | ID, version, maturity stage, date, authors present |
| Article IDs valid                          | Article identifiers follow the defined scheme      |
| Constitutional Compliance Statement        | Statement included and non-empty                   |
| GRF Register initialized                   | GRF register for this review cycle is present      |
| GOV-000 traceability update included       | GOV-000 update artifact is present in package      |
| Cross-reference validation report included | Cross-reference report is present in package       |

**On failure:** Issue GRF-D findings for missing metadata items; issue GRF-A if the Constitutional Compliance Statement or GRF Register is absent.

### Gate 2 — Constitutional Conformance

**Purpose:** Verify that the artifact faithfully implements CONST-001 without scope expansion or contradiction.

| Check                               | Pass Condition                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Faithful implementation             | All constitutional requirements delegated to this artifact are addressed |
| No scope expansion                  | Artifact does not claim authority beyond its constitutional mandate       |
| No internal contradictions          | No clause contradicts any clause in CONST-001                            |
| No conflicts with ratified siblings | No clause contradicts any C3-ratified Tier 1 artifact                   |

**On failure:** Scope expansion or contradiction with CONST-001 → GRF-A. Minor misalignment not rising to contradiction → GRF-B.

### Gate 3 — Structural Integrity

**Purpose:** Verify that the artifact's internal model is coherent and enforceable.

| Check                     | Pass Condition                                                          |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organizational model      | Authority relationships are defined and non-circular                    |
| Authority classes         | Authority classes are distinct, complete, and mutually exclusive        |
| Responsibility flow       | Responsibilities are assigned to exactly one authority class            |
| Independence constraints  | Independence requirements from CONST-001 are preserved                  |
| Lifecycle events          | All maturity transitions and lifecycle events are defined               |
| Organizational invariants | Invariants are stated, testable, and consistent with constitutional baseline |

**On failure:** Circular authority or unassigned responsibility → GRF-A. Structural ambiguity → GRF-B. Unclear but non-critical structure → GRF-C.

### Gate 4 — Traceability

**Purpose:** Verify that every constitutional requirement delegated to this artifact is mapped in GOV-000, and that delegation integrity is maintained.

| Check                             | Pass Condition                                                      |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GOV-000 mappings present          | Every requirement implemented by this artifact is mapped in GOV-000 |
| Cross-reference validation pass   | Cross-reference report shows no unresolved conflicts                |
| Delegation integrity              | No requirement is delegated to more than one artifact               |
| No orphaned requirements          | No constitutional requirement is unmapped                           |

**On failure:** Unmapped constitutional requirement → GRF-A. Duplicate delegation → GRF-B. Incomplete cross-reference report → GRF-B.

### Gate 5 — Governability

**Purpose:** Verify that the artifact is operationally enforceable and measurable, and does not leak into operational concerns outside its constitutional scope.

| Check                          | Pass Condition                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enforcement defined            | Each requirement specifies an enforcement mechanism or authority             |
| Compliance metrics measurable  | Each requirement can be assessed as compliant or non-compliant               |
| No operational leakage         | Artifact does not specify operational procedures that belong in lower tiers  |
| Ratification pathway defined   | The artifact states its own ratification and amendment pathway               |

**On failure:** No enforcement mechanism for a requirement → GRF-A. Unmeasurable compliance → GRF-B. Operational leakage → GRF-C.

### Gate 6 — Disposition

**Purpose:** Issue a formal disposition based on the aggregate findings from Gates 1–5.

Disposition is issued after all five preceding gates are complete. The disposition is determined by the GRF classification rules in § 7 and the blocking rules in § 8.

---

## 7. GRF Classification

All findings identified during a review are recorded as Governance Review Findings (GRFs) in the GRF Register for the artifact under review.

| Classification | Name                            | Definition                                                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **GRF-A**      | Blocking Constitutional Defect  | A defect that violates CONST-001, creates scope expansion, introduces contradiction, leaves a constitutional requirement unimplemented, or removes enforcement. Blocks maturity advancement until resolved. |
| **GRF-B**      | Structural Concern              | A structural issue that does not rise to a constitutional defect but requires correction before or concurrent with ratification. Does not block C1→C2 advancement but must be resolved before C2→C3. |
| **GRF-C**      | Improvement Recommendation      | A non-blocking improvement recommendation that strengthens the artifact. Recorded for tracking but does not block advancement. |
| **GRF-D**      | Editorial or Clarification Item | A minor editorial correction, wording clarification, or metadata fix. Recorded for tracking but does not block advancement. |

Each GRF record MUST include:

- GRF ID (format: `GRF-[A|B|C|D]-[ARTIFACT]-[NNN]`)
- Gate at which the finding was raised
- Description of the finding
- Evidence source (E1–E6) supporting the finding
- Required resolution action

---

## 8. Blocking Rules

### 8.1 GRF-A Blocking Rule

**Any open GRF-A finding blocks maturity advancement.**

- An artifact with one or more unresolved GRF-A findings MUST receive a disposition of **Revise** or **Reject**.
- GRF-A findings must be resolved and the artifact resubmitted before re-review.
- Resolution of a GRF-A finding requires evidence of correction referenced in the resubmission package.

### 8.2 GRF-B Non-Blocking Rule

- GRF-B findings do not block advancement from C1 to C2.
- GRF-B findings MUST be resolved before advancement from C2 to C3 (Ratified).
- Unresolved GRF-B findings at C2 are documented in the Tier 1 Ratification Report.

### 8.3 GRF-C and GRF-D

- GRF-C and GRF-D findings never block advancement.
- They are recorded in the GRF Register and tracked for resolution at the discretion of the owning authority.

## 9. Review Output Format

Each completed review MUST produce a **Governance Review Record** containing the following sections:

```
Governance Review Record
========================
Artifact:              [Artifact ID and name]
Version:               [Maturity stage and draft label]
Review Date:           [ISO 8601 date]
Protocol:              ADR-0002
Reviewer:              [Reviewer role or designation]

Gate Results:
  Gate 1 – Completeness:               [PASS | FAIL]
  Gate 2 – Constitutional Conformance: [PASS | FAIL]
  Gate 3 – Structural Integrity:       [PASS | FAIL]
  Gate 4 – Traceability:               [PASS | FAIL]
  Gate 5 – Governability:              [PASS | FAIL]

Governance Score:        [Numeric or qualitative score, if adopted]
Blocking Findings:       [Count of open GRF-A findings]
Non-Blocking Findings:   [Count of GRF-B, GRF-C, GRF-D findings]
GRFs Issued:             [List of GRF IDs]

Disposition:
  [ ] Approve
  [ ] Approve with Findings
  [ ] Revise
  [ ] Reject
  [ ] Defer

Recommendation:          [Narrative rationale for the disposition]
```

### 9.1 Disposition Definitions

| Disposition              | Meaning                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Approve**              | All five gates pass. No GRF-A findings. Artifact may advance to the next maturity stage.                       |
| **Approve with Findings**| All five gates pass. No GRF-A findings. One or more GRF-B, GRF-C, or GRF-D findings are recorded.            |
| **Revise**               | One or more GRF-A findings. Artifact must be revised and resubmitted. Maturity stage does not advance.         |
| **Reject**               | Fundamental constitutional defect. Artifact requires redesign. Maturity stage resets.                          |
| **Defer**                | Review cannot be completed due to dependency on an incomplete upstream artifact or unresolved external factor.  |

## 10. Maturity Advancement Logic

Maturity advancement is governed by the following rules:

| Stage Transition | Requirement                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C0 → C1          | Author declares artifact as draft and places it in the governance repository.            |
| C1 → C2          | Disposition of **Approve** or **Approve with Findings** under this protocol. No open GRF-A findings. |
| C2 → C3          | All GRF-B findings from the C1→C2 review are resolved. Integrated Tier 1 Review complete (§ 11). |
| C3               | Artifact is **Ratified**. No further structural changes permitted without a new ADR.     |

### 10.1 Maturity Stage Definitions

| Stage | Name             | Description                                                                |
| ----- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C0    | Planned          | Artifact is planned but not yet drafted.                                   |
| C1    | Draft            | Artifact is drafted and submitted for governance review.                   |
| C2    | Review Candidate | Artifact has passed initial governance review. Candidate for ratification. |
| C3    | Ratified         | Artifact is ratified and frozen. Amendments require a new ADR.             |

## 11. Integrated Tier 1 Review

After all four Tier 1 constitutional artifacts (CONST-001, CONST-002, CONST-003, CONST-004) reach **C2**, an **Integrated Tier 1 Review** MUST be conducted before any artifact advances to C3.

The integrated review evaluates questions that only emerge when artifacts are examined as a system:

| Question                         | Purpose                                                                          |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Authority boundary consistency   | Are authority boundaries consistent and non-overlapping across all Tier 1 artifacts? |
| Constitutional requirement conflicts | Do any constitutional requirements conflict when artifacts are read together? |
| Exclusive delegation             | Does every delegated responsibility have exactly one implementing artifact?       |
| Duplicate mandates               | Are any mandates claimed by more than one artifact?                              |
| Governance gaps                  | Are there constitutional requirements not owned by any artifact?                 |
| GOV-000 completeness             | Does GOV-000 provide complete traceability from every constitutional requirement to its implementing artifact? |

### 11.1 Output

The integrated review MUST produce a **Tier 1 Ratification Report** documenting:

- Aggregate GRF status across all Tier 1 artifacts
- Resolution of any inter-artifact conflicts identified
- Confirmation that GOV-000 is complete
- A formal recommendation on the readiness of the constitutional layer as a whole

The Tier 1 Ratification Report is a prerequisite for the **Governance Baseline v1.0.0** repository tag.

### 11.2 Founding Week Completion Criteria

Founding Week is considered complete when all of the following conditions are simultaneously true:

| Criterion                  | Condition                                                  |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tier 1 ratification        | All four constitutional artifacts are at C3 (Ratified)     |
| Traceability complete      | GOV-000 maps every constitutional requirement to its implementing artifacts |
| Cross-validation pass      | Integrated Tier 1 Review passes with no open GRF-A findings |
| Zero open GRF-A findings   | No unresolved GRF-A findings across any Tier 1 artifact    |
| Constitutional Readiness   | Constitutional Readiness Dashboard shows 100%              |
| Repository baseline tagged | Repository is tagged as **Governance Baseline v1.0.0**     |

## 12. Consequences

### 12.1 Positive Consequences

- All Tier 1 governance reviews are produced by a versioned, auditable method.
- Review records are directly comparable across artifacts and review cycles.
- Future reviewers can perform reviews consistently without relying on ad hoc judgment.
- GRF classification creates a clear distinction between blocking and non-blocking issues.
- The integrated review requirement ensures system-level coherence before ratification.

### 12.2 Negative Consequences / Trade-offs

- Review packages must include GOV-000 updates and cross-reference reports, increasing submission overhead.
- The six-gate sequence adds structure that may feel heavyweight for simple artifacts. This overhead is accepted as the cost of governance assurance at the constitutional tier.
- Any change to this protocol requires a new ADR, creating a lightweight coordination cost for protocol evolution.

### 12.3 Risks

| Risk                                   | Mitigation                                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reviewer interprets gates inconsistently | Evidence hierarchy (§ 5) anchors all findings to cited sources.    |
| GRF-A over-use stalls governance       | GRF-A is reserved for constitutional defects; structural concerns are GRF-B. |
| Integrated review delayed indefinitely | Integrated review is triggered by C2 completion, not by schedule.   |
