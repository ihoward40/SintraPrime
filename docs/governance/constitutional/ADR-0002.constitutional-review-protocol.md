# ADR-0002 — Constitutional Review Protocol

## Status

Adopted

## Purpose

Define the locked review sequence and disposition rules for Tier 1 constitutional artifacts.

## Review sequence

1. **Completeness Review**
2. **Constitutional Conformance**
3. **Structural Integrity**
4. **Traceability**
5. **Governability**
6. **GRF Classification**
7. **Formal Disposition**

## Protocol rules

- Reviews are evidence-based and cite the governing artifact under examination.
- Reviewer independence is preserved between artifact authorship and disposition.
- Findings are recorded in the GRF register before disposition is finalized.
- Disposition states use the lifecycle vocabulary defined in `governance-lifecycle-vocabulary.v1.md`.
- A failed gate returns the artifact to remediation without altering the locked sequence.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Mission and constitutional artifact under review
2. Adopted ADRs
3. Governance audit baseline
4. Traceability matrix
5. Readiness dashboard and verification logs

## Disposition outputs

- **Advance** — artifact cleared to the next governance step.
- **Remediate** — artifact must correct identified defects before re-review.
- **Hold** — external dependency or missing evidence prevents a fair disposition.

## Freeze boundary

This ADR does not authorize new templates or governance models during review unless a genuine constitutional, traceability, or governability defect requires remediation.
