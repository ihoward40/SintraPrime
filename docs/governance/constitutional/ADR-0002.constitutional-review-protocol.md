## 1. Purpose

ADR-0002 establishes a permanent, repeatable, and versioned protocol for reviewing constitutional,
governance, operational, and implementation artifacts within SintraPrime Enterprise. This protocol
defines who may conduct reviews, what evidence is required, what review gates exist, how findings
are classified, how dispositions are issued, how disagreements are resolved, and how review records
are retained.

This document governs the **review method**. It does not redefine constitutional principles,
organizational authority, or the constitutional hierarchy established in CONST-001 and CONST-003.
Those remain the exclusive province of the constitutional documents themselves.

Adoption of this protocol makes the governance review process independently auditable, consistent
across artifact types, and resilient to reviewer turnover.

## 3. Review Authority

Reviews are conducted by **designated reviewers** appointed under the authority chain established in
CONST-001 → CONST-003. Review authority is role-based, not individual-based.

| Role                    | Authority                                              |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Constitutional Reviewer | Reviews Tier 1 artifacts; issues Tier 1 dispositions   |
| Governance Auditor      | Validates traceability; issues GRF-B through GRF-D     |
| Escalation Authority    | Resolves contested findings; issues final disposition  |

No reviewer may conduct a review of an artifact for which they are also the primary author.
This independence constraint is normative and may not be waived below the Escalation Authority level.

---

## 4. Review Lifecycle

The following lifecycle is **immutable** for all artifact reviews governed by this protocol. Stages
must be executed in the order shown. No stage may be skipped or reordered unless superseded by a
future ADR adopted under the amendment procedure in Section 12.

```
Submission
    ↓
Completeness Review
    ↓
Constitutional Review
    ↓
Structural Review
    ↓
Traceability Review
    ↓
Governability Review
    ↓
Disposition
    ↓
GRF Issuance
    ↓
Closure
```

### Stage Descriptions

| Stage                | Purpose                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Submission           | Reviewer receives the artifact package and confirms it is ready to review |
| Completeness Review  | Verify all required sections, metadata, IDs, and companion documents are present |
| Constitutional Review | Confirm the artifact faithfully implements or is consistent with CONST-001 without contradiction or scope expansion |
| Structural Review    | Assess authority hierarchy, responsibility flow, independence constraints, and structural invariants |
| Traceability Review  | Validate GOV-000 mappings, cross-reference integrity, and delegation chain |
| Governability Review | Confirm every binding article or rule includes enforcement and measurable compliance metrics with no operational leakage |
| Disposition          | Issue one of the normative dispositions defined in Section 8              |
| GRF Issuance         | Record all findings using the GRF format defined in Section 7             |
| Closure              | Archive the review record under the template in Section 10                |

---

## 5. Review Gates

Each review stage has a mandatory pass condition. A stage is considered passed only when all
conditions for that stage are satisfied. Any failed condition produces a finding classified under
Section 7.

| Gate                 | Pass Condition                                                              |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Completeness         | All required sections present; metadata complete; article IDs valid; companion documents included |
| Constitutional       | Faithfully implements or is consistent with CONST-001; no scope expansion; no contradictions |
| Structural           | Organizational model complete; authority classes defined; responsibility flow intact; independence constraints preserved; lifecycle events defined |
| Traceability         | GOV-000 mappings present; cross-references validated; delegation chain intact; no orphaned requirements |
| Governability        | Every binding rule includes: rule, delegation, enforcement, compliance metric; no operational leakage into constitutional scope |

## 6. Evidence Requirements

Reviews are evidence-based. Opinion without evidence is not a valid basis for a finding, disposition,
or escalation.

Evidence is evaluated in the following priority hierarchy:

1. **Constitution (CONST)** — Governing authority; supersedes all other evidence
2. **Charters (CONST)** — Organizational and governance structure
3. **Manuals (MAN)** — Responsibilities and controls
4. **Playbooks (PLAY)** — Operational execution
5. **Implementation** — Agents, automations, repositories, code

Where implementation conflicts with governance, governance prevails until formally amended through
the appropriate constitutional or ADR process.

### Required Submission Package

```yaml
Submission:
  Artifact: <artifact identifier and version>
  Included:
    - Primary artifact document
    - Constitutional Compliance Statement
    - GRF Register (open items)
    - GOV-000 Traceability Update
    - Constitutional Readiness Dashboard (if applicable)
    - Cross-Reference Validation Report
```

## 7. Finding Classification

All findings produced during a review are classified as Governance Review Findings (GRFs) using
the following normative level table.

| Level | Name                  | Description                                                                         | Blocks Approval |
| ----- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| GRF-A | Constitutional Defect | A direct contradiction of or non-compliance with CONST-001 or the applicable constitutional authority | **Yes — blocking** |
| GRF-B | Structural Defect     | A material defect in structure, authority, traceability, or governability that requires correction before the artifact is production-ready | **Typically blocking** |
| GRF-C | Improvement           | A recommendation that would improve clarity, completeness, or quality but does not prevent advancement | No |
| GRF-D | Editorial             | A clarification, formatting, or wording item with no substantive governance impact  | No |

**Blocking behavior:**

- **GRF-A** always blocks maturity advancement. The artifact must be revised and re-reviewed.
- **GRF-B** blocks advancement unless the Escalation Authority grants a documented waiver with a
  mandatory GRF closure timeline.
- **GRF-C** and **GRF-D** are recorded in the review record and tracked but do not block advancement.

---

## 8. Disposition Rules

Each review produces exactly one disposition from the following normative vocabulary:

| Disposition            | Meaning                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Approve**            | All gates passed; no blocking findings; artifact may advance to the next maturity level |
| **Approve with Findings** | All gates passed; one or more GRF-C or GRF-D findings recorded; advancement permitted |
| **Revise**             | One or more GRF-A or GRF-B findings identified; artifact must be revised and resubmitted |
| **Reject**             | Fundamental constitutional defects that cannot be resolved through revision; artifact must be redesigned |
| **Defer**              | Review cannot be completed due to missing evidence or unresolved external dependency; artifact returned to submitter |

A disposition of **Revise** or **Reject** requires the reviewer to provide a written finding
rationale for each blocking GRF before the artifact may be resubmitted. Redesign is not
permitted as a response to a non-blocking finding.

## 9. Escalation Process

Any party with standing in the review may escalate a contested finding or disposition.

### Grounds for Escalation

- Reviewer applied incorrect constitutional authority
- Evidence was present but excluded from the review
- Finding is not supported by the cited constitutional basis
- Disposition is inconsistent with the findings on record

### Escalation Procedure

1. The contesting party submits a written escalation to the Escalation Authority, citing the
   specific finding ID, the constitutional basis for the contest, and the evidence presented.
2. The Escalation Authority reviews the contest within the timeframe defined in the applicable
   governance schedule.
3. The Escalation Authority issues one of: **Uphold**, **Modify**, or **Overturn**.
4. The escalation decision and rationale are appended to the review record.
5. The escalation decision is final within the scope of this protocol and may only be challenged
   through a constitutional amendment.

## 10. Review Records

Every completed review produces a standardized review record. Records are append-only and must
be retained for the lifecycle of the artifact.

### Standard Review Record Template

```yaml
Review-ID: <unique identifier, e.g., REV-CONST-002-R1>
Artifact: <artifact identifier>
Version: <artifact version under review>
Reviewer: <reviewer role and identity>
Review-Date: <ISO 8601 date>

Disposition: <one of: Approve | Approve with Findings | Revise | Reject | Defer>

Governance-Score: <numeric or qualitative score, if applicable>

Review-Gates:
  Completeness: <Pass | Fail>
  Constitutional: <Pass | Fail>
  Structural: <Pass | Fail>
  Traceability: <Pass | Fail>
  Governability: <Pass | Fail>

GRFs:
  - ID: <GRF identifier>
    Level: <GRF-A | GRF-B | GRF-C | GRF-D>
    Gate: <review gate where finding was identified>
    Finding: <description of the finding>
    Constitutional-Basis: <CONST-001 article or ADR section cited>
    Resolution-Required: <Yes | No>

Recommendation: <narrative recommendation for next steps>

Next-Action: <specific action required: Resubmit | Advance | Close | Escalate>
```

## 11. Review Quality Metrics

The governance review process is itself subject to quality measurement. The following metrics are
tracked to evaluate the health of the review process over time.

| Metric                               | Description                                                   |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Average review turnaround            | Time from submission to disposition issuance                  |
| Findings per artifact                | Mean number of GRFs issued per reviewed artifact              |
| False-positive findings              | Findings overturned on escalation as a proportion of total    |
| Reopened reviews                     | Reviews reopened after initial closure                        |
| GRF closure time                     | Time from GRF issuance to verified closure                    |
| Cross-reference validation success   | Proportion of cross-reference checks passing without finding  |

These metrics are reported to the Governance Auditor on the cadence defined in CONST-003 or its
delegated operational artifact.

## 13. Amendment Procedure

Amendments to this protocol are governed as follows:

1. Propose the amendment as a new GRF-C or GRF-B finding against the current version, or as a
   new ADR if the change is architectural.
2. The amendment must be reviewed under this same protocol before adoption.
3. Amendments that alter the lifecycle flow (Section 4), finding classification (Section 7), or
   disposition rules (Section 8) require Escalation Authority approval.
4. CONST-001 freeze behavior is **not** affected by amendments to this protocol. Improvements to
   constitutional freeze behavior must be submitted as GRFs, not as edits to CONST-001.
5. Upon adoption, the version number in Section 12 is incremented according to the versioning
   convention, and the amendment rationale is recorded in the version history table.

## Binding Review Principles

The following principles are normative. They take precedence when a reviewer must exercise
judgment in the absence of an explicit rule.

1. **Evidence before opinion** — Findings must be grounded in documented evidence, not reviewer preference.
2. **Constitution before implementation** — When evidence conflicts, constitutional authority supersedes implementation artifacts.
3. **Traceability before approval** — No artifact advances without a verified, complete traceability chain.
4. **Findings before redesign** — Redesign is not a response to a non-blocking finding.
5. **Consistency before optimization** — Consistent application of the protocol takes precedence over optimizing individual review outcomes.
6. **Governance before velocity** — Review thoroughness is not sacrificed for speed of delivery.
