# ADR-0001 — Tier 1 Constitutional Freeze

```yaml
Decision-ID:  ADR-0001
Title:        Tier 1 Constitutional Freeze
Status:       Accepted
Effective:    Founding Week
Decided By:   Constitutional Reviewer & Governance Auditor
```

---

## Context

CONST-001 Enterprise Constitution has reached maturity level C2 (Governance Review Candidate).
The remaining Tier 1 constitutional artifacts — CONST-002, CONST-003, and CONST-004 — are
either in draft or planned state.

Continuing to modify CONST-001 while authoring subordinate artifacts creates constitutional
churn: each improvement to CONST-001 may require cascading revisions to documents that
implement it, lengthening Founding Week indefinitely.

The architecture is mature enough that the primary risk is no longer design — it is
implementation drift between CONST-001 and its implementing artifacts.

---

## Decision

CONST-001 v1.0-r2 is frozen as the Governance Review Candidate (C2).

**No structural changes are permitted to CONST-001 except defect corrections.**

---

## Rationale

1. Preventing constitutional churn allows subordinate artifacts to be authored against a
   stable baseline.
2. The freeze boundary is clear and enforceable: defects (corrections to existing intent)
   are permitted; amendments (new intent) are not.
3. New constitutional requirements discovered during CONST-002, CONST-003, and CONST-004
   authoring are captured as Governance Review Findings, preserving their traceability
   without destabilizing CONST-001.
4. All accepted GRFs are incorporated into CONST-001 v1.0-r3 before ratification.

---

## Consequences

- No structural changes to CONST-001 except defect corrections approved via GRF process.
- New constitutional requirements discovered during CONST-002/CONST-003/CONST-004 authoring
  are logged as Governance Review Findings (GRFs).
- Accepted GRFs are incorporated into CONST-001 v1.0-r3 immediately before ratification.
- This ADR is referenced in the CONST-001 freeze notice and the GRF Register header.

---

## Compliance

Any change to CONST-001 while this ADR is in Accepted status requires:
1. A GRF documenting the defect.
2. Governance Review Board approval.
3. A minor version increment (r2 → r3 or equivalent).

*End of ADR-0001*
