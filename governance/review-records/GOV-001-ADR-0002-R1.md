## GRF Findings (Non-Blocking)

### GRF-C-001 — Reviewer Qualification

**Subject:** Reviewer qualification requirements  
**Level:** GRF-C (non-blocking)  
**Recommendation:** Specify minimum reviewer qualifications.  
**Rationale:** Protects the integrity of future reviews if additional reviewers join.

```
Reviewer must:
  - Understand Tier hierarchy
  - Understand constitutional scope
  - Be independent (no stake in artifact under review)
  - Have completed governance onboarding
  - Possess review authority
```

**Resolution:** Applied to ADR-0002 v1.1.0 — Section 4 updated.
