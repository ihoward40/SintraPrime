# GOV-MAT — SintraPrime Governance Maturity Model

## Purpose

This document defines the five-level governance maturity model for SintraPrime Enterprise. It provides a calibrated benchmark for measuring governance development across the Founding Week and beyond. Every constitutional and governance artifact is assessed against this model during GRF review cycles.

---

## Maturity Levels

### Level 0 — Uninitialized

**Description:** No governance artifacts exist. Operations are ad hoc. No authority model, no audit trail, no compliance measurement.

**Criteria:**
- No constitutional documents
- No defined authority model
- No documented policies
- No review process

**Constitutional Equivalence:** Pre-constitutional state. Exit gate blocked.

---

### Level 1 — Scaffolded

**Description:** Governance framework is established. Constitutional scaffold adopted. Artifact IDs assigned. Review process defined. No Tier 1 artifacts approved yet.

**Criteria:**
- GOV-000 adopted
- GRF-PROC adopted
- ADR-0001 in effect
- At least one CONST artifact in Draft (C1) state

**Founding Week Target:** Achieved at start of constitutional drafting.

---

### Level 2 — Constitutional Draft

**Description:** Tier 1 constitutional documents exist in draft form and are under active review. No artifacts approved. GRF registers initialized.

**Criteria:**
- CONST-001, CONST-002 (and required peers) in Draft or Under Review
- Review packages open
- GRF registers initialized
- No open Critical findings blocking review

**Founding Week Target:** All Tier 1 drafts complete by end of Day 1–2.

---

### Level 3 — Constitutionally Approved

**Description:** All Tier 1 documents approved at v1.0. Cross-references validated. Founding Week Exit Gate passed.

**Criteria:**
- CONST-001 through CONST-004 at Approved v1.0
- All cross-references validated
- GRF Round 1 closed with no open Critical/High findings
- Governance review signed

**Founding Week Target:** End of Founding Week. Required for any production operations.

---

### Level 4 — Operationally Governed

**Description:** Tier 2 governance manuals approved and actively used. RACI, Approval Matrix, Security Policy, Repository Standards in effect. Agents operating under governed constraints.

**Criteria:**
- Tier 2 artifacts at Approved v1.0
- Agents operating under Agent Handbook
- Approval gates enforced
- Compliance measurable

**Post-Founding Target:** First operational cycle after Founding Week.

---

### Level 5 — Continuously Governed

**Description:** Full governance stack active. Tier 3 operational playbooks in effect. Hermes Company Pulse reporting weekly. GRF reviews on cadence. Constitutional amendments processed through formal protocol.

**Criteria:**
- All 16 artifacts at Approved v1.0 or later
- Regular governance reviews on schedule
- Company Pulse reporting active
- No sustained non-compliance without escalation resolution

**Long-Term Target:** Ongoing operational standard.
