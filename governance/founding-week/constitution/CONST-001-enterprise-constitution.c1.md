# SintraPrime Enterprise Constitution

---

# Preamble

## Section 1. Purpose

This Constitution establishes the supreme governing authority of SintraPrime Enterprise and defines the enduring principles, authority model, constitutional controls, enforcement framework, and amendment process by which the enterprise is governed.

## Section 2. Authority

This Constitution is issued under the authority of the Founder & CEO and ratified through the constitutional approval process defined herein.

## Section 3. Scope

This Constitution governs all business units, departments, agents, automations, repositories, integrations, workflows, and governed operations conducted under SintraPrime Enterprise.

## Section 4. Definitions

Authoritative definitions are maintained in Artifact #16 — Enterprise Glossary, incorporated by reference.

## Section 5. Constitutional Principles

All enterprise activity shall conform to the Constitutional Principles set forth in Book III.

---

# Book I — Enterprise Identity

## Article 1. Mission

**Purpose:** Establish the enterprise's governing statement of purpose.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** SintraPrime Enterprise exists to build, govern, and scale AI-enabled systems, products, and services through disciplined execution, measurable outcomes, and constitutional governance.

**Delegation:** Business unit missions are defined in Appendix C and subordinate charters.

**Enforcement:** Any business unit or operational mission inconsistent with the enterprise mission requires constitutional review.

**Compliance Metric:** All approved business unit charters are consistent with this enterprise mission statement.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** The mission intentionally emphasizes governance as co-equal to technical execution. This prevents the enterprise from building fast at the cost of governing poorly.

---

## Article 2. Vision

**Purpose:** Establish the enterprise's long-term aspirational direction.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** SintraPrime Enterprise operates as a durable platform where intelligent systems and human leadership produce reliable, auditable, and compounding value across multiple business units.

**Delegation:** Business unit visions are defined in subordinate charters.

**Enforcement:** Strategic plans inconsistent with this vision require constitutional review before adoption.

**Compliance Metric:** Strategic plans reference and align to this vision statement.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** The vision emphasizes durability and auditability over speed. Compounding value implies that governance investments today reduce friction tomorrow.

---

## Article 3. Values

**Purpose:** Establish the binding value framework for all enterprise decisions.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Enterprise decisions, operations, and conduct shall conform to these constitutional values:
1. Accountability
2. Integrity
3. Clarity
4. Security
5. Evidence-based action
6. Operational excellence
7. Continuous learning

**Delegation:** Application of values to operational decisions is defined in subordinate governance manuals.

**Enforcement:** Decisions demonstrably inconsistent with these values are subject to governance review and escalation.

**Compliance Metric:** GRF reviews assess value alignment of governance artifacts.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** These values are not aspirational preferences — they are constitutional constraints. Operations that systematically contradict them are subject to constitutional challenge.

---

## Article 4. Business Units

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional recognition of enterprise business units.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** SintraPrime Enterprise recognizes multiple business units operating under shared constitutional governance. All business units are subordinate to this Constitution.

**Delegation:** Business unit details, mandates, and governance bindings are maintained in Appendix C and subordinate charters.

**Enforcement:** Unrecognized or unregistered business units may not operate under enterprise authority.

**Compliance Metric:** All active business units are registered in Appendix C.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Initial business units include SintraPrime Technologies, IKE SOLUTIONS, and LegalOS. Appendix C is the authoritative registry.

---

## Article 5. Executive Authority

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional basis for executive authority.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Executive authority originates with the Founder & CEO and is delegated according to this Constitution and subordinate governing artifacts.

**Delegation:** Authority delegation model is defined in Book IV. Operational authority is defined in subordinate governance manuals.

**Enforcement:** Exercises of authority without constitutional basis are void.

**Compliance Metric:** All authority exercises traceable to a constitutional delegation chain.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Authority without delegation is not authority — it is ad hoc action. Constitutional traceability is what makes authority legitimate.

---

## Article 6. Human Authority

**Purpose:** Establish the supremacy of human decision authority over agentic systems.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Human decision authority is supreme over automated and agentic systems. No agent or automation may supersede authorized human decision rights.

**Delegation:** Human authority levels and decision rights are defined in Book IV and Artifact #8 Approval Matrix.

**Enforcement:** Any automated action that overrides a human decision without explicit delegated authority is a constitutional violation.

**Compliance Metric:** Zero verified incidents of agent actions overriding human authority without explicit delegation.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** This article applies regardless of agent capability. Capability does not confer authority.

---

# Book II — Governance

## Article 1. Organizational Structure

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional requirement for a governed organizational structure.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** The enterprise shall maintain a governed organizational structure. The structure is defined, reviewed, and amended through formal governance process.

**Delegation:** Organizational structure details are defined in CONST-002 — Organizational Charter and implemented through subordinate manuals.

**Enforcement:** Organizational changes without Organizational Charter amendment are unauthorized.

**Compliance Metric:** Organizational structure is current and consistent with approved CONST-002.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** The Constitution establishes that an organizational structure must exist and be governed. CONST-002 defines what that structure is.

---

## Article 2. Department Authority

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional basis for department authority.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Departments derive authority from this Constitution and shall operate within defined constitutional and delegated boundaries.

**Delegation:** Department responsibilities, authority bounds, and accountability are defined in Artifact #5 — Department Handbook, Artifact #7 — RACI Matrix, and Artifact #8 — Approval Matrix.

**Enforcement:** Departments acting outside their defined authority are in constitutional violation.

**Compliance Metric:** Department actions traceable to defined authority in subordinate artifacts.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Department authority is delegated, not inherent. Departments may not unilaterally expand their authority.

---

## Article 3. Agent Authority

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional basis and constraints for agent authority.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Agents operate only within explicitly delegated authority and constraints. Undelegated agent action is unauthorized.

**Delegation:** Agent authority, permissions, and operational constraints are defined in Artifact #6 — Agent Handbook, Artifact #8 — Approval Matrix, Artifact #9 — Security Policy, and Artifact #10 — Repository Standards.

**Enforcement:** Agents acting outside delegated constraints are in constitutional violation. Human oversight applies at all times.

**Compliance Metric:** Zero verified instances of agent actions outside delegated authority boundaries.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Agent capability does not define agent authority. Only explicit constitutional delegation defines what an agent may do.

---

## Article 4. Decision Rights

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional requirement for documented decision rights.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Decision rights shall be explicit, documented, and consistently applied. Undocumented decision rights are invalid.

**Delegation:** Decision right details are defined in subordinate artifacts including Artifact #7 RACI Matrix and Artifact #8 Approval Matrix.

**Enforcement:** Decisions made under undocumented authority are subject to constitutional review and potential reversal.

**Compliance Metric:** All protected decisions traceable to documented decision rights.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Ambiguous decision rights are a primary source of governance failure. Constitutional clarity prevents disputes and unauthorized action.

---

## Article 5. Delegation

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional rules for authority delegation.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Delegated authority must be explicit, documented, reviewable, and revocable. Undocumented delegation is constitutionally invalid.

**Delegation:** Delegation mechanisms and limits are defined in subordinate governance manuals.

**Enforcement:** Claimed authority based on undocumented delegation is void.

**Compliance Metric:** All active delegations documented and reviewable.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** The ability to revoke delegation is as important as the delegation itself. Irrevocable delegation is incompatible with constitutional governance.

---

## Article 6. Escalation

**Purpose:** Establish the constitutional requirement for escalation pathways.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Escalation pathways are mandatory for unresolved risk, authority conflicts, policy conflicts, security concerns, and protected actions.

**Delegation:** Escalation procedures are defined in subordinate governance manuals and the Approval Matrix.

**Enforcement:** Failure to escalate when required is a governance violation.

**Compliance Metric:** All escalation-required events have documented escalation records.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Escalation is not failure — it is the governance mechanism for handling situations beyond delegated authority. Bypassing escalation is the actual failure.

# Book III — Constitutional Principles

The following principles are binding across all enterprise activity. No automation, workflow, or decision may violate them.

| Principle | Binding Statement |
|---|---|
| 1. Human Oversight | All automated and agentic systems operate under active human oversight. |
| 2. Transparency | Enterprise operations are transparent to authorized reviewers and auditors. |
| 3. Auditability | All governed actions produce auditable evidence. |
| 4. Evidence Before Action | Decisions require documented evidence proportional to their risk. |
| 5. Least Privilege | All actors and systems operate with the minimum authority required. |
| 6. Separation of Duties | Control functions are separated from execution functions. |
| 7. Security by Default | All systems default to secure configurations without explicit opt-out. |
| 8. Standards Before Automation | Automation is built on approved documented standards. |
| 9. Governance Before Expansion | Governance capability must precede operational expansion. |
| 10. Constitutional Supremacy | No action, automation, or procedure may conflict with this Constitution. |

## Principle of Constitutional Supremacy

**No automation, AI agent, workflow, repository change, integration, or operational procedure may conflict with this Constitution. Where conflict exists, this Constitution governs until formally amended.**

# Book VIII — Versioning

## Article 1. Version Policy

**Purpose:** Establish constitutional versioning standards.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Constitutional versions follow formal semantic constitutional versioning:
- Major version: structural or authority-model changes
- Minor version: clarifications or additions without structural disruption
- Patch: non-substantive corrections

**Delegation:** Version management is defined in Artifact #10 Repository Standards.

**Enforcement:** Version changes without appropriate classification are a governance violation.

**Compliance Metric:** All published versions carry correct version classification.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** The version number is a constitutional signal. A major version change signals that something fundamental changed.

---

## Article 2. Amendment Process

**Purpose:** Establish the formal process for constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Amendments require: (1) written proposal, (2) impact assessment, (3) governance review, (4) constitutional quorum approval, (5) formal publication and effective date.

**Delegation:** Quorum definition is delegated to CONST-003 Governance Charter.

**Enforcement:** Changes to constitutional artifacts without completed amendment process are unauthorized under ADR-0001.

**Compliance Metric:** All constitutional version changes traceable to completed amendment records.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** The amendment process is what makes the Constitution stable while remaining adaptable.

---

## Article 3. Approval Quorum

**Purpose:** Establish the quorum requirement for constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Approval quorum for constitutional amendments is defined in CONST-003 Governance Charter and must be met.

**Delegation:** Quorum composition and voting process defined in CONST-003.

**Enforcement:** Amendments approved without quorum are void.

**Compliance Metric:** Amendment approvals carry documented quorum verification.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Until CONST-003 is approved, the Founder & CEO serves as the quorum of one for constitutional amendments.

---

## Article 4. Review Schedule

**Purpose:** Establish mandatory review cadence.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** This Constitution shall undergo scheduled review at least annually, or sooner upon triggering events defined in subordinate governance policy.

**Delegation:** Review scheduling is administered by the COO (Hermes) per CONST-003.

**Enforcement:** Overdue reviews are escalated to Executive Council.

**Compliance Metric:** Constitution reviewed within 12 months of last review date.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Annual review does not mean annual revision. Most reviews should confirm the Constitution remains fit for purpose.

---

## Article 5. Supersession

**Purpose:** Establish the supersession mechanism.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** A new approved version supersedes prior versions as of its effective date. Superseded versions are archived, not deleted.

**Delegation:** Archive management defined in Artifact #10 Repository Standards.

**Enforcement:** Superseded versions may not be used as authoritative governance references.

**Compliance Metric:** All superseded versions archived with clear supersession notation.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** Archiving, not deletion, preserves constitutional history for audit and legal purposes.

---

## Article 6. Emergency Amendments

**Purpose:** Establish emergency amendment authority.

**Constitutional Rule (Binding):** Emergency amendments may be enacted under expedited authority for material legal, security, or existential business risk, with mandatory retrospective review within 30 days.

**Delegation:** Emergency authority definition is in CONST-003 Governance Charter.

**Enforcement:** Emergency amendments without retrospective review within 30 days become void.

**Compliance Metric:** Zero emergency amendments without completed retrospective review within 30 days.

**Commentary (Non-Binding):** The retrospective review requirement is what prevents emergency authority from becoming a routine bypass of constitutional process.
